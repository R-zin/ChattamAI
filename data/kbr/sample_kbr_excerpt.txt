PART A -- APPLICABILITY, PLOT AREA AND BUILT-UP AREA

Rule 5   Applicability and minimum plot
  5.1    These rules apply to all building construction, reconstruction and
         addition within the local-self-government (LSGD) area to which they
         extend, for the occupancy categories defined in Rule 12.

  5.2    The minimum plot area required for a residential building of Group A1
         occupancy shall be 3 cents (about 121.4 square metres). Accessory
         buildings and additions to existing structures are exempt from this
         minimum plot requirement.

  5.3    Plot area means the area of the whole parcel of land including the
         portion occupied by the building. Built-up area means the area covered
         by the building measured to the outer face of the external walls,
         excluding open verandas, balconies and projecting cornices that do not
         rest on the ground. The total built-up area of all floors shall not
         exceed the floor space index limit in Rule 7 applied to the plot area.


PART B -- FLOOR SPACE INDEX (FSI), NUMBER OF FLOORS AND HEIGHT

Rule 7   Floor space index, number of floors and height
  7.1    Floor Space Index (FSI) is the ratio of the total built-up area of all
         floors to the plot area. For Group A1 (residential) occupancy on a
         plot of 240.0 square metres, the maximum permissible FSI shall be 2.0
         where the abutting road width is not less than 7.0 metres.

  7.2    Worked example for this sample: for a plot area of 240.0 square metres
         and a permissible FSI of 2.0, the maximum total built-up area is
         240.0 x 2.0 = 480.0 square metres across all floors.

  7.3    The maximum number of floors for a Group A1 residential building under
         this sample shall be 3 floors. A building of 3 floors with a total
         built-up area of 480.0 square metres gives an average of 160.0 square
         metres per floor, which is within the FSI limit of rule 7.1.

  7.4    Building height means the vertical distance measured from the average
         ground level abutting the building to the highest point of the roof.
         For a Group A1 residential building of not more than 3 floors the
         maximum building height shall be 11.0 metres.


PART C -- SETBACKS (FRONT, REAR AND SIDES) AND ROAD WIDTH

5.3 Setbacks (open spaces) for Residential (Group A1) buildings
  5.3.1  The minimum front setback from the plot boundary abutting the road
         shall be 3.0 metres.

  5.3.2  The minimum rear setback from the plot boundary opposite the road
         shall be 2.0 metres.

  5.3.3  The minimum side setback from each side boundary shall be 1.5 metres.
         Where a side abuts a common passage of width not less than 1.0 metre,
         the side setback on that side may be relaxed to 1.0 metre.

  5.3.4  Road width means the width of the public or private street abutting
         the plot, measured between the plot boundaries on either side. A
         minimum road width of 7.0 metres is required to avail the FSI of 2.0
         in rule 7.1; for roads narrower than 7.0 metres but not less than 4.5
         metres the permissible FSI is reduced proportionately.


PART D -- PARKING AND OCCUPANCY

11.2 Parking
  11.2.1 For every residential dwelling unit of built-up area exceeding 150.0
         square metres, a minimum of one car-parking space of not less than
         12.5 square metres per space shall be provided within the plot or
         within the building footprint.

  11.2.2 Parking spaces provided in the open at ground level shall not be
         counted towards the built-up area for the purpose of FSI in rule 7.1,
         provided they remain permanently open to the sky and are used only
         for parking.

Rule 12  Occupancy classification
  12.1   Buildings are classified by occupancy. Group A1 means residential
         occupancies, including one-family and two-family dwelling units and
         apartment houses where sleeping accommodation is provided for normal
         residential purposes, with or without cooking or dining facilities.

  12.2   The occupancy classification determines the applicable FSI, height,
         setback, parking and fire-safety provisions in these rules. A change
         of occupancy requires the whole building to conform to the provisions
         for the new occupancy.


PART E -- FIRE SAFETY AND STAIRCASE NOTE

Rule 16  Fire safety and staircase
  16.1   A residential building having more than two floors, or a building
         height exceeding 7.5 metres, shall be provided with not less than one
         enclosed staircase of minimum width 1.2 metres leading to a place of
         refuge or open space at ground level.

  16.2   For a building of more than three floors, a second independent
         staircase shall be provided. The maximum travel distance from any
         occupied room to the nearest staircase exit shall not exceed 22.5
         metres.

  16.3   Fire safety note for this sample: the values above are illustrative
         of KBR practice for low-rise residential buildings. Buildings of
         higher occupancy classes or greater height are additionally subject
         to the National Building Code and approvals from the Fire and Rescue
         Services. This is a SAMPLE excerpt -- confirm against the official
         rules for the actual occupancy and height of any plan under review.
